# Roadmap

## v0.2 (current)

Shipped in v0.1:

- AI System Inventory (full CRUD + Excel export + EU AI Act auto-suggestion)
- AI Impact Assessment (10-section wizard + Word export)
- AI Risk Register (CRUD + heatmap + Excel export)
- ISO 42001 roadmap (static)
- Privacy page, methodology, about, contribute, data management

Added in v0.2:

- Vendor Assessment module — 40 weighted questions across 8 domains
  (governance, data, security, model lifecycle, fairness, transparency,
  compliance, continuity), red-flag detection, live scoring, Word export
- Maturity Self-Assessment — 40 questions across 8 domains, radar chart,
  action-plan builder, Word export
- All 12 templates written and generated by `scripts/generate-templates.ts`
  (charter, AUP, intake, model card, board deck, quarterly report, ethics
  code, incident response plan, risk appetite, human oversight spec, data
  subject rights, vendor termination)
- Cross-link to AI-Controls-Catalog — risk register AI-CTRL references
  render as live links
- Cross-link to AI-RedTeam-Framework — inventory carries optional
  `lastRedTeamDate` / `lastRedTeamNotes`
- ISO 42001 roadmap: interactive per-phase progress tracking, persisted to
  localStorage and included in the export bundle
