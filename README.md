[**Start your AI inventory →**](https://aigovernancetoolkit.dev/inventory)

## What's inside (v0.1)

- 🗂️ **AI System Inventory** — classify, register, govern with EU AI Act
  risk classification (auto-suggested)
- 📋 **AI Impact Assessment (AIIA)** — per ISO/IEC 42005, generates a
  professional Word output
- 🎯 **AI Risk Register** — heatmaps, owner workflows, Excel export
- 🗺️ **ISO/IEC 42001 Implementation Roadmap** — 52-week phased plan
- 📄 **6 downloadable templates** (charter, AUP, intake, model card, board
  deck, quarterly report) — 6 more in v0.2

All open. All free. **All your data stays in your browser.**

## Why this exists

Every regulated enterprise in 2026 is being asked: "What's our AI governance
program?" Most are pasting templates from random consultancies and
half-implementing ISO 42001. This toolkit is the operating kit they need —
interactive, exportable, audit-defensible, ISO 42001-aligned.

## Standards alignment

- ISO/IEC 42001:2023 — AI management systems
- ISO/IEC 23894:2023 — AI risk management
- ISO/IEC 42005 — AI system impact assessment
- NIST AI RMF 1.0
- EU AI Act (Regulation (EU) 2024/1689)

## Privacy

All user-entered data is stored **only in your browser** via localStorage. No
backend. No accounts. No telemetry on data content. See [PRIVACY.md](PRIVACY.md).
